# Live model audit — integrated observations and dispositions

## Phase and scope

The bounded live model audit is integrated at the **candidate-observation** stage. All 17 scoped operations have explicit dispositions: **14 sampled endpoints**, **3 skipped endpoints**, and **15 body observations** (the 14 sampled operations include two separately recorded `GetStatusLogs` contexts). The sample artifacts contain **14 operation models** and **15 observation labels**.

The exact source IDs, routes, methods, sample/skipped status, per-operation comparison and review resolutions are in [`evidence/live-audit/path-dispositions.json`](../evidence/live-audit/path-dispositions.json). The original scope is [`evidence/guardian-batch/scope.json`](../evidence/guardian-batch/scope.json); redacted source observations are in [`evidence/live-audit/observed-shapes.json`](../evidence/live-audit/observed-shapes.json) and the operation candidate records.

## What was integrated

- The **one canonical static response correction** is `GetEvents`: its HTTP body root is now an array. The old `items` property was the local `action.data.items` envelope slot holding the whole HTTP body. The static array item keeps all **17 direct source-read fields / 23 total field nodes including nesting**, open, optional, and source-typed as unknown where not proven. See [`evidence/responses/communications.json`](../evidence/responses/communications.json) and [`models/responses.tsp`](../models/responses.tsp).
- `GetRecentEvents` remains a static open object with root `events` and `count` typed `unknown`; the successful client trace does not establish their types or nested item reads. Its sample-observed `events: array` and `count: integer`, plus the captured item path/type shape, live only in the context view.
- Wider observed paths and types for the sampled operations are represented by [`models/live-observations.tsp`](../models/live-observations.tsp) and [`scripts/observed-response-models.json`](../scripts/observed-response-models.json), generated from the redacted observations by `scripts/generate.mjs`. These artifacts are explicitly sample-only, open, optional, and non-normative; they are **not linked to API operations or normative OpenAPI**. Null-only values stay unknown in the sample view. They preserve the one-account shapes/types without publishing values.
- Wider sample views are not claims that the corresponding partial static fields were wrong. They capture fields not reached by the traced static consumer paths. Preserve the legitimate static-only fields and branches even where one sample omits them.

## Runtime, privacy, and contract limits

The sampled `HTTP 200` statuses and media labels are observation metadata only; error/alternate response branches were not probed. Observed sample types, nulls, empty arrays/objects, counts, equality outcomes, and coarse date descriptors are not guarantees of requiredness, universal types/nullability, enums, defaults, completeness, stable identifiers, access policy, date semantics, or server behavior. All permissions values were omitted; 32 observed permission keys being integers in this one sample does not prove Boolean or enum semantics. No real dates, IDs, credentials, cookies, names, address contents, event descriptions, menu text, or diary content are recorded.

Only authorized authentication/session creation and bounded account-discovered reads are represented. The two login POSTs are the explicit exception; all other sampled endpoints are GET. GET method and absence of an observed explicit mutation do not prove absence of access logging or other server-side effects. No feature writes, seen/read requests, unrelated enumeration, staff-roster substitution, secondary staff diary reads, retries, or error probes are included. Raw captures remain outside the repository.
